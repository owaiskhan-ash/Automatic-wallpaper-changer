Your photos,

Pick a handful of photos, choose how often they should change, and forget about it. AutoWall reshapes every shot to your screen — rounded corners, colour wash, lock screen — and swaps it in the background with no ads, no accounts and no drama.

Installing

  1. Download the APK and open it on your phone.
  2. Allow “install unknown apps” for your browser when Android asks.
  3. Pick photos, flip the switch, done.
